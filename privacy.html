Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026 · Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how Exquisite Home Decor, the operator of this website, handles information in connection with the website and its planned personal Pinterest publishing tool.

For privacy questions or requests, contact mwcoig@gmail.com.

1. Current website and project status

This is a new home-decor and kitchen-ideas project. The public website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not currently offer visitor accounts, collect payments, provide a contact form or mailing list, or install visitor analytics, advertising pixels, or social-media tracking widgets.

The Pinterest integration is in development and is not currently available as a public service. The planned integration below describes its intended operation if Pinterest grants access. We will update this policy before introducing materially different data practices.

2. Information handled when you visit

We do not currently set our own tracking cookies or browser storage on the public website. GitHub, our hosting provider, logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Hosting-related information is handled by GitHub under its own policies; we do not control GitHub's logs or retention periods.

See the GitHub Privacy Statement for more information.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond and handle your request. Please do not email passwords, access tokens, identity documents, or payment details.

3. Planned Pinterest integration

The planned tool is for the operator's own Pinterest business account. Website visitors will not be asked to connect their Pinterest accounts or provide Pinterest passwords, session cookies, or access tokens.

If approved and authorized, the tool will use Pinterest's authorization process and only the permissions needed to select the operator's boards, create or schedule individually approved Pins, and view the operator's own permitted analytics. Information involved may include the authorized account identifier, board identifiers, Pin content and links, authorization credentials, and performance metrics that Pinterest permits the tool to access.

Account access is intended to support the operator's publishing and reporting workflow. Pinterest data will not be sold, shared with advertising networks, used to profile website visitors, or used to train or improve AI or machine-learning models without Pinterest's express permission. The tool will not collect other users' account information for unrelated purposes.

Credentials will be kept outside the public website and repository. Pinterest data will be retrieved and retained only as permitted by Pinterest's developer terms. The tool will not maintain a separate archive of API-returned Pins or board data where Pinterest prohibits storage.

4. Affiliate links and external websites

This project may publish affiliate links to retailers or other affiliate partners. If you follow a link and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a commission. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Clicking an external link takes you to a service operated by someone else. Retailers, Pinterest, and affiliate providers may receive information such as your IP address, browser details, referring page, and affiliate attribution identifiers, and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. Their privacy practices, checkout processes, and retention periods are outside our control. Affiliate disclosures will also be provided with promotional content as appropriate.

Relevant policies include the Pinterest Privacy Policy and Amazon Privacy Notice. We do not receive your payment card details from affiliate purchases.

5. Use and disclosure

We use information we receive to operate the project, answer requests, maintain security, and comply with applicable obligations. Hosting providers, email providers, and authorized platform services process information necessary to provide their services. We do not sell personal information or disclose it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or legal claims.

6. Retention and security

We keep correspondence only as needed to respond, resolve the matter, and meet applicable recordkeeping or legal obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence using the contact address above. If a record must be retained, we will explain the applicable limitation.

For any future Pinterest connection, access credentials will be removed when the connection is discontinued; permitted retained data will be deleted when no longer needed or when required by Pinterest's terms. Providers may retain their own records under their respective policies. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to this small project, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you or request access, correction, or deletion. Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have additional privacy rights. We will handle requests as required by applicable law and may need proportionate verification before disclosing or deleting information.

You can control cookies through your browser. The operator can revoke a future Pinterest connection through Pinterest's account settings and request removal of locally retained connection information. Disconnecting an app does not itself delete Pins already published on Pinterest; those can be managed on Pinterest.

8. Children and international processing

The website and planned tool are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information to this project.

GitHub, email providers, Pinterest, and affiliate providers may process information in the United States or other countries according to their own policies and applicable safeguards.

9. Changes and contact

We will publish changes on this page and update the date above. For questions, privacy requests, or concerns, email mwcoig@gmail.com.
